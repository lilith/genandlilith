Our Story

Gen and Lilith met online, and went on their first date on August 15th, 2023. They went to brunch at a cute restaurant and then had a spa day; they had so much fun they ended up getting dinner together, and then they went to Lilith's house so Gen could see her 3D printed spice racks and giant playset for her kids.

They hit it off so well that for their second date Gen met Lilith's kids, Jason and Ian, and for their third date Lilith met Gen's family over Mexican food.

Gen and Lilith both have a passion for engineering, travel, family, food, and adventure so they hit it off right away. They so enjoyed each others company that their 4th date was a trip to Mexico together where Gen asked Lilith to officially be her girlfriend. Things took off from there and they started sharing life together, family events, trips to Japan, Europe, and beyond, Lilith's first camping trip, and so much more!

The following year they finally took a trip to Paris together that they had talked about on their very first date. On that trip during a visit to Versailles in the gardens near the temple of love, Lilith proposed to Genevieve, to which she received a very enthusiastic yes.

FAQs

Will there be parking available?

Yes! Parking is available on site at the venue. There is handicap parking, much of the normal parking will be a bit of a walk, however there will be a golf cart picking people up to take them from their cars to the venue so please know that's an option!

Can I bring my kids?

Yes, we ourselves have kids that will be attending, so we do plan to welcome any kids of family and friends that they would like to bring along. Don't want to bring them or prefer to get a sitter? No worries! If you do wish to bring them along just make sure to include them in your RSVP along with ages for our final guest count please.

Will there be vegetarian food options?

Yes! Dinner will be buffet style with several options for main courses and many different options for various sides and salads as well. Eat whatever sounds good to you or meets any dietary restrictions you may have. If there's something particular you think we should be aware of or that requires special prep you can mention it in the RSVP or reach out to one of us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Can I RSVP before receiving my invitation?

Yes, absolutely! We still are planning to send out invites, but if you know you'll be coming please RSVP. We would also love for you to share your current address, email, and phone with us at zola.com/addr/9YyZZ8V6Y
